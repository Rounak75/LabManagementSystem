# Lab Management System (Admin)

A medical laboratory management system administration dashboard used by lab managers and admins to manage pricing, users, analytics, and business rules.

## Mode
Operate

## Core Jobs
- Managing the Test Catalogue and pricing.
- Managing user roles and clinic configurations.
- Viewing system-wide analytics.

## User Constraints
- Users often have high cognitive load. Interfaces must be extremely clean, reducing visual noise.
- Medical context requires high trust and professionalism.
